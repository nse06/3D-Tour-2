The three options

Today (build 6)

Photos painted on RoomPlan's boxes

Runs on the phone in seconds. Walls and floors look right; furniture is a box, so a sofa's cushions and a chair's legs smear across flat faces.

Build 7 (shipping now)

Photos painted on the LiDAR mesh

Still on the phone, still seconds. Furniture and clutter keep their real shape from the iPhone's LiDAR; edges of thin parts (chair legs, lamp poles) are rough.

Photoreal (cloud GPU)

Gaussian splats trained on the photos

Hundreds of thousands of soft, colored blobs fitted to the photos. Reflections, window views and clutter come out the way the camera saw them. Needs a GPU in the cloud.
